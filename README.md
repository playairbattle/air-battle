# Air Battle

**Three planes. One opponent. Your strategy.**

Air Battle is a browser-based, turn-based aerial strategy game. Two players place their fleets on a 10 × 10 board and use hits and misses to locate the opponent's aircraft.

**Outthink. Outplay.**

This is the official public information page for Air Battle. It contains documentation only. The game implementation, smart contract source code, deployment configuration and internal project files are not published in this repository.

## Technology Stack

- Browser-based game with EVM blockchain integration.
- BNB Smart Chain mainnet is a supported production network.

## Features

- Turn-based, one-versus-one strategy.
- Three aircraft per player on a 10 × 10 board.
- Hits and misses provide clues to the opponent's formation.
- A hit on an aircraft's nose destroys that aircraft.
- Free practice against the bot; paid matches require an entry.
- Private rooms and community matchmaking.
