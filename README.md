## 🧭 Your flight plan

Air Battle is a **browser-based, turn-based aerial strategy game for two players**. Hide your formation, read the clues and find your opponent's fleet before they find yours.

1. **🛩️ Deploy your fleet.** Place three aircraft on your 10 × 10 board. Your opponent cannot see your formation.
2. **📡 Scan the radar.** Choose a cell on the opposing board and take your shot.
3. **🧩 Read the clues.** Use hits and misses to work out where the aircraft are hidden.
4. **🎯 Find the nose.** A nose hit destroys the entire aircraft. Destroy all three to win.

**Ready for your first sortie?** [Open the Flight Academy →](https://play.air-battle.com/?view=guide&lang=en)
